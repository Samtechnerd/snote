SNote

Handwriting notebooks for your e-paper or Android tablet, with a web app that keeps everything in sync.

Open the web app Download for Android

What it does

Stylus-first ink

Pen, marker and eraser with pressure. Palm rejection means only the pen writes.

Notebooks and folders

Covers, tabs, one-level folders and fixed A4 pages, laid out like a real notebook.

Typed pages and text boxes

Type with headings, lists, bold, italic and more, or drop text boxes onto a handwritten page.

Import and mark up PDFs

Bring in a PDF, name it, file it in a folder and write over it like any notebook.

Export to PDF

Turn any notebook into a PDF with your ink and text on top.

Colour or e-paper

Light and dark colour themes, plus a high-contrast e-paper mode with a refresh control.

Sign in and sync

One account across devices. Notebooks, pages and imported PDFs follow you.

Web app

Open, write in and add to your notebooks from a browser, including an iPad with Apple Pencil.

Page lock

One tap stops fingers from panning or zooming the page while you write.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your tablet (about 16 MB).
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
  3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unrecognised, choose to install anyway.
  4. Open SNote and sign in to sync, or skip it and use the app on its own.

Needs Android 8 or newer. SNote isn't on the Play Store, so updates are installed by downloading the file again over the top.

Use it in the browser

Sign in with the same account to see your notebooks. It works on a computer or an iPad, and Apple Pencil writes while fingers are ignored.
